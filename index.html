FAQ

For other questions, call the clinic during opening hours.

Does it hurt?

We explain what to expect before treatment and check in as we go. For some fillings, we can use the Solea laser without a needle or drill. You can ask us to pause at any point.

What if my child is scared?

We start with what your child feels ready for. That might mean sitting in the chair and taking a look around. We can pause and plan another visit together.

Do you bill my insurance directly?

Yes. We bill most private plans directly. Bring your insurance card so we can confirm coverage and any amount you’ll owe before treatment.

How fast can you see us for a toothache?

We reserve same-day appointments for dental emergencies during opening hours. Call by 10 a.m. so we can check availability.

Do you accept the Canadian Dental Care Plan?

Yes. Please bring your CDCP card. We’ll confirm your coverage and any amount you’ll owe before treatment.
